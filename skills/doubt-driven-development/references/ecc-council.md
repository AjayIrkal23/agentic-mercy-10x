# Council: anti-anchoring (ECC graft)

When more than one reviewer weighs a decision:

- Each reviewer writes its own verdict and reasons BEFORE seeing anyone else's.
- Reviewers get the same inputs (the diff, the claim, the evidence), never another verdict.
- The synthesiser compares verdicts only after all are in; disagreements are listed, not
  averaged away.
- A reviewer that saw another's verdict first is discarded and re-run fresh.
