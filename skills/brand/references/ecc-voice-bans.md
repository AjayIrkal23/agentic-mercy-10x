# Voice profile and hard bans (ECC graft)

## Voice profile fields

- audience (who reads it, what they already know);
- personality (three adjectives, each with an "and not" — "confident, not arrogant");
- vocabulary (words we use, words we avoid);
- sentence shape (length, person, contractions yes/no);
- examples (one on-voice and one off-voice sample per channel).

## Hard bans

Hype words without evidence ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "world-class"); claims
without a source; exclamation marks in product copy; jargon the audience field rules out;
anything that contradicts a legal or compliance note.
